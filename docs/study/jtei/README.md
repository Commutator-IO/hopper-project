# jTEI article

`article.tex` / `article.pdf` — *A Machine Reads the Hopper Ledgers: Constrained
Transcription, Mechanical TEI Export, and Checkability as the Editorial
Condition*, a research paper of about 7,500 words in the body, prepared for the
*Journal of the Text Encoding Initiative* and drawn from the critical note in
the folder above. Structure: introduction, related work, corpus, transcription
method, TEI encoding (markup examples from the edition's own export and the
ODD's element list), interface and reproducibility, discussion (transcription
against image, limits of the model, human validation), conclusion, the
generative-AI declaration jTEI requires, data availability, references, and
two appendices (a complete transcribed leaf; a subset of the derived tables).

Rebuild with Tectonic: `tectonic article.tex`. The figures are the note's
`fig-*.tex` regenerated by `../tex/figures.mjs`; the tables subset is cut from
`../tex/tables.tex`. For submission the figures have to be exported as PNG and
the article encoded in the jTEI TEI schema; both steps are on issue #25.
