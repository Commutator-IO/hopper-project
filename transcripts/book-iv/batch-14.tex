% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-09 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book IV, batch 14: sheets 157 to 161, the last five of the volume. Four are
% written leaves - 156 to 159 - and the fifth is the back cover. They carry two
% years and three months, from 17 December 1964 to 23 March 1967, and the last
% of them is the last entry in Book IV. Batch 13, sheets 145 to 156, has no
% transcription, so this file does not continue the one before it: leaves 144
% to 155 stand between them and have not been read.
%
% Eight things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% The columns are still the stationer's and still unheaded, as they have been
% since leaf 7, and the hand of the entries is the one hand of the earlier
% batches. Nothing on these sheets identifies the writer, so the batch is
% attributed to no one.
%
% The colour rule holds and then doubles. Charges are black, receipts red and
% sums pencil, as they have been since leaf 7. What is new is on leaf 158,
% where two passages are written twice, one ink over the other: « Dec. 15, 1966
% Balance on Chair Car » stands in blue with the same words traced over it in
% black, and the three lines below it - « to be deposited by John in Bank of
% New York on Jan 1, 1967 » - stand in red with the same words traced over them
% in black, word for word and slightly out of register. Neither is a
% correction. Nothing is added or altered in the second writing.
%
% The linked-loop 0 is the trap of the batch, and it is the one batch 10 warned
% about. Twice a figure reads as an 8 letter by letter and is a 0: leaf 157's
% « Chair Car » payment reads 10,800.00, and the glyph after its comma is the
% same one she uses for the first 0 of the 6,000.00 written two lines below,
% where 10,000 and 6,000 make the 16,000.00 the leaf rules off and the cheque
% pays; leaf 158's figure above the red receipt reads 18,666.67 and the receipt
% immediately under it reads 10,666.67, which is what 16,666.67 less 6,000.00
% gives. Both are transcribed as 0 and both are noted on the leaf.
%
% One figure will not settle. Leaf 158's commission line reads 8398.83 at
% magnification - her 3 carries a closed upper loop, which « less 33 1/3 » on
% the same line shows, and three of its digits close far enough to read as 9
% and 8. A third of 25000.00 is 8333.33, and the 16666.67 ruled off below it is
% legible and is 25000.00 less 8333.33. The figure is given as the arithmetic
% wants it and marked doubtful, and the note says what the glyphs say.
%
% The accounts run across the leaf-turn and across the year end, and they
% close. Leaf 157's cheque of 16,000.00 on 18 December 1965 pays on account for
% two pictures at once, « Chair Car » for Dr and Mrs David B. Fall and
% « Intermission » for Howard Mack; leaf 158 carries the balance of Intermission
% forward, settles it on 1 July 1966, and then receipts the balance of Chair Car
% on 15 December 1966 with a note that the cheque is to be deposited on 1
% January 1967; leaf 159 opens 1967 by entering that deposit again as its first
% item. The same 8,000.00 is therefore written on two leaves and counted in two
% years, and the book says so both times - the same device as the cheques
% returned to John Clancy on leaves 133 to 135.
%
% The buyers are named in the entries themselves. Batch 12 found the volume's
% first buyers in a pencil margin on leaf 133; here they are in the body of the
% record, in the same ink as everything else - Daniel Leshner, Detroit, a Dr
% Levitt whose forename will not be read, Dr and Mrs David B. Fall, Howard Mack
% - and two institutions pay reproduction fees, the Whitney for « Early Sunday
% Morning » and the Museum of Modern Art for « House by a Railroad » on a book
% jacket.
%
% Expenses come off a sale for the first time in the volume. Leaf 156 rules off
% at 21000.00, then lists relining, cleaning and varnishing of « East River », a
% frame by Kulicke for the same picture and a frame by Granick for the
% Charleston houses, and takes 372.00 off the net before the cheque. The leaf
% spells the picture two ways eight lines apart: « House at Charlestown » in the
% sale and « Houses at Charleston » in the frame line.
%
% The volume ends without saying so. The last entry is an Artex royalty of 23
% March 1967 on « Light House a 2 lights », receipted twice at two figures a
% fortnight apart; the rest of leaf 159 is empty, and the back cover carries no
% writing of any hand.

\marginal{Total 21,944.00 --- in pencil, to the right of the money column and
across the two lines below the December receipt, with a second pencil line
under it rubbed out and illegible}

\note{the four columns are the stationer's and not the writer's, as they are
throughout the volume: the leaf comes ruled with a red vertical rule at the
left for the date and two more at the right dividing dollars from cents, and
no heading is written above any of them anywhere in this book. They are
therefore left unheaded here, in every leaf of the batch.}

\note{the entry's arithmetic closes down to the receipt. 6500 and 25000 make
the 31500 ruled off; a third of it is the 10500 deducted; the net is 21000; the
three expenses of 190, 144 and 38 make the 372 ruled off below them; and 21000
less 372 is 20628, which is the figure written twice at the foot in black and
in red.}

\note{both figures of 20628.00 are written into a blot and retraced --- the
black one has a rubbed grey patch over its cents and the red one is written
across a spread of its own ink --- and neither can be read digit by digit.
They are given as the subtraction above them gives them and marked doubtful on
that account.}

\note{the first word of the expenses is « Rel- » followed by a run of minims and
a g. « Relining » fits the number of strokes and the operation the line goes on
to describe; the minims will not be counted with certainty at any
magnification.}

\note{« House at Charlestown » in the sale and « Houses at Charleston » in the
frame line are eight lines apart on this leaf, and both are as written.}

\note{the pencil total of 21,944.00 is a year total for 1964 and takes in more
than this leaf: 20628.00 is all the leaf receipts in that year. Leaf 155, which
carries February to November 1964, has not been transcribed.}

\note{no decimal point is written in the Artex charge of 33 37; the figure
straddles the cents rule and is set here in the two columns as the rules divide
it. The red receipt of 6.63 is written with a point and is smaller than the
charge above it, and nothing on the leaf explains the difference.}

followed by four or five minims and a descending g. Nothing at 1000\% will
separate the minims, and no reading is offered. The surname reads « Levitt »
with « Lewitt » defensible at the third letter.}

after the comma is the closed-loop 0 batch 10 recorded: it is the same shape as
the first figure after the comma in the 6,000.00 two lines below, and 10,000
with 6,000 is the 16,000.00 the leaf rules off and the cheque pays.}

\note{the December cheque settles two accounts in one payment, and both are
payments on account rather than the whole price. Leaf 158 carries the balance
of each forward.}

\note{« Dec. 15, 1966 » and « Balance on Chair Car » are written in blue and
then written again in black over the blue, word for word and a little out of
register. The three lines beginning « to be deposited » are written in red and
then written again in black over the red in the same way. Nothing is added or
altered in either second writing, and nothing on the leaf says who made it.}

\note{the commission figure reads 8398.83 at magnification. Her 3 in figures is
made with a closed upper loop --- « less 33 1/3 » on the same line shows it ---
and three of the digits here close far enough to be read as 9 and as 8. A third
of 25000.00 is 8333.33 and the 16666.67 ruled off below is legible, so the
figure is given as 8333.33 and marked doubtful. The glyphs themselves do not
settle it.}

\note{the figure above the red receipt reads 18666.67 and is the same
closed-loop 0 as on leaf 157: 16666.67 less the 6000.00 payment on account
above it is 10666.67, which is what the red receipt under it pays.}

\note{the date at the head of the receipt line is written, struck through, and
written again immediately to its right, both times in red and both times
« July 1 ».}

\note{the leaf is empty below « Jan 1, 1967 ». The 8,000.00 receipted here on 15
December 1966 is entered again as the first item of 1967 on leaf 159.}
