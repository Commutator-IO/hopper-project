% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-09 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book IV, batch 10: sheets 109 to 120, which are leaves 108 to 119, and carry
% just under eight years from 1 January 1938 to 5 December 1945. Every sheet in
% the batch is a written leaf; there are no covers, no insertions, no blanks and
% no clippings.
%
% Nine things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% The columns have not changed and are still unheaded, as they have been since
% leaf 7, and the hand is the one hand of the nine earlier batches. Nothing on
% these twelve sheets identifies the writer, so every ledger passage is
% \hand{unidentified}. Two pencil annotations on leaf 116 are in a different,
% smaller and more upright pencil hand and are attributed no more confidently.
%
% The book states its own rule about what counts as income, and it is the first
% time in ten batches that it has. On leaf 116 a Kennedy print sale of 14
% January 1944 - charged 30, commission 10, receipted 20 - carries « Take note »
% in pencil beside the client and « not included in income. » with a pencil
% cross in the money column beneath it. Leaf 108 had already applied the rule
% without writing it: four museums refund Hopper's travel expenses for jury duty
% that spring - 56.04, 8.20, 78.60, 18.95 - every one of them charged and
% receipted in full, and the leaf's foot sum is 600, which is the single Rehn
% cheque and none of the refunds. A reimbursement is not a sale, and neither is
% a print sold at a price the writer did not want counted.
%
% « Less commission » stops naming the commission, three times, and the
% arithmetic is what catches it. On leaf 117's Rehn entry of 15 June 1944 the
% line reads « less commission 733.34 » against 1100 of items - but 733.34 is
% 1100 less a third, so the figure written is the net and not the deduction, and
% the next line takes 6.40 of photographs off it to reach the cheque of 726.94.
% Leaf 116's Allison entry does the same in miniature: 30 charged, « less
% commission 20 », cheque 20. And leaf 112's Rehn entry of 27 December 1940 goes
% the other way - 930 of items, « less commission and photos 24 », net 596 -
% where 24 is the photographer's bill alone and the third, 310, is not written
% at all. All three stand as written.
%
% Where it is written, the commission is a flat third and the leaves say so:
% 300 of 900, 190 of 570, 250 of 750, 1583.33 of 4750, 2275 of 6825, 600 of
% 1800, 1016.66 of 3050. Only twice does it depart, and both times downward by a
% cent - 519.99 of 1560 on leaf 112, 666.66 of 2000 on leaf 115. The Library of
% Congress is the one client charged a rate in writing, « less commission 25\% »,
% and the one charged anything but a third.
%
% The sums are the best run in the volume. Eight leaves carry one and all eight
% close to the cent: 108 (600), 109 (3561.21), 111 (2823.66), 113 (1320), 114
% (4537.44), 115 (3140.54), 118 (1120), and leaf 110, which carries two - a
% pencil 2160.47 closing 1939 and a foot sum of 1018.50 opening 1940, both
% exact. Leaf 114 carries a second figure as well, « 1942 --- 5857.44 » written
% out in the description column, which is its own 4537.44 plus leaf 113's 1320:
% the first year total in Book IV to be struck across two leaves and labelled
% with its year. Leaf 119 does the same for 1945 in pencil, 3624.24, which is
% leaf 118's 1120 plus this leaf's 2004.24 and 500.
%
% Three leaves carry no sum - 112, 116 and 117 - and 1944 is the loser. Leaf 116
% rules a pencil line under the last 1943 receipt and writes no figure above it;
% leaf 117 runs its two large Rehn entries to the middle of the leaf and stops;
% leaf 118 opens with November 1944 and then rules off at « 1945 » without
% totalling what stands above. So the year of Hotel Lobby and the six water
% colours is never added up anywhere in the book.
%
% One entry disagrees with itself, in the shape batches 8 and 9 found. Leaf
% 109's Rehn entry of 14 May 1938 lists 3800, 1000 and 750, which make 5550, and
% rules off 4750 beneath them. The commission of 1583.33 is a third of 4750, the
% cheque is 3166.67, and the leaf's foot sum of 3561.21 uses the cheque - so
% subtotal, commission, cheque and leaf sum all side with 4750 and only the three
% items disagree, by exactly 1000. The 8 of 3800 is a narrow double loop and
% matches the 8 of the red 180 seven lines below; it has not been read down to
% suit the subtotal.
%
% Five habits of notation change in these leaves. The dollar sign reaches the
% money column - batch 8 found the first one in a description on leaf 94, and
% here $900, $596, $854.60, $1200 and $1657.07 are written in the column itself,
% on leaves 112, 113 and 114. The decimal point arrives with it, so that
% 1182.16, 666.66, 12.80, 726.94 and 1.31 are written across the ruled division
% instead of on either side of it. A round sum in the cents column is a linked
% double o - two joined loops, not two figures - which is what 28, 20, 400, 800,
% 1000 and 3000 carry here. The medium is marked before or after the title by a
% letter: « o » for oil, « w.c. » for water colour, and on leaf 119 « E » for
% etching, which is new. And a brace joins two titles to one figure on leaf 117,
% the first in the volume.
%
% The clients are a gallery, a mint and a jury circuit. Rehn takes almost
% everything and the figures are the largest in the book - 3166.67, 2317.84,
% 1971, 1328.14, 1657.07, 2004.24 and 4357.76, which is six water colours, two
% oils and a drawing on 14 April 1944 and the largest single cheque in Book IV.
% Prints go on selling at 30 less a third to Keppel, Kennedy, Weyhe, Babcock,
% Allison and the Philadelphia Museum. Beside them the money now comes from
% institutions: the Library of Congress, the Virginia Museum of Fine Arts, the
% Pennsylvania Academy, the John Herron Art Institute, the Worcester Museum's
% divided prize, the Art Institute of Chicago twice - the Ada S. Garrett Prize
% of 750 for Night Hawks in 1942 and the Logan Prize of 500 in 1945 - the
% Salmagundi cash prize of 1000, and royalties from Associated American Artists,
% Living American Art and the Am. Artists Group. The last two entries in the
% batch are not sales at all: « Cooperative Interest » of 1.31 and « Rebate on
% Liability Ins » of 10.38, both in December 1945.
