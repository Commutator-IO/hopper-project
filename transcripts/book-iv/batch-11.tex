% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-09 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book IV, batch 11: sheets 121 to 132, which are leaves 120 to 131, and carry
% eight years and one month from 4 April 1946 to 7 May 1954. Every sheet in the
% batch is a written leaf; there are no covers, no insertions, no blanks and no
% clippings.
%
% Eight things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% The columns are still the stationer's and still unheaded, as they have been
% since leaf 7, and the hand is the one hand of the ten earlier batches. Nothing
% on these twelve sheets identifies the writer, so the batch is attributed to no
% one. The colour rule holds throughout: charges in black, receipts in red, leaf
% and year sums in pencil.
%
% The book is now three or four entries a leaf and one of them is nearly all the
% money. Rehn takes 2994.40, 2347.17, 1800, 500, 2856.02, 4213.03, 1666.67,
% 1788.81, 3175.15, 3500, 1000, 2706.85, 2666.67, 1833.34, 3945.77, 3894.32,
% 2666.66, 2323.34 and 2000; everything else in eight years is a print at 20 or
% 30, a jury honorarium at 100 or 150, a reproduction fee at 10, and royalties
% of 3.48 to 42.40. Two entries are on account and name nothing at all - 3500 on
% 10 May 1951 and 1000 on 21 December - and one refers the reader elsewhere:
% « refer to statement of Dec 29, 1951 » on leaf 128, the first entry in Book IV
% to cite a document outside the book.
%
% A receipt line written across the row that already carried the subtotal, then
% rubbed out and written again below, is now a habit rather than an accident.
% Batch 10 found five; this batch has four more, on leaves 121, 122, 124 and
% 130, each in the same shape - the ditto and the day still legible in pale red,
% « rec'd » or « total » in traces, the rest gone. They are transcribed on the
% subtotal's row, so that the leaf does not gain a line it does not have.
%
% « Less commission » names the net rather than the deduction three times more,
% and the arithmetic is again what catches it. Leaf 130's Rehn entry of 16 June
% 1953 reads « less expenses and commission 3894.32 » against 4000.02, and
% 3894.32 is the cheque; leaf 131 does it twice on one leaf, « less 33 1/3 \%
% commission 2666.66 » against 4000 and « less 33 1/3 Commission 1166.66 »
% against 3500, and both figures are receipted. Where the commission is genuinely
% the deduction it is a flat third to the dollar - 900 of 2700, 250 of 750, 2250
% of 6750, 833.33 of 2500, 1596.65 of 4790, 1983.33 of 5950 - and only leaf 124
% departs, by a cent downward, 919.99 of 2760.
%
% Four leaves disagree with themselves, and no figure has been adjusted to make
% one close. Leaf 122 rules off 305.49 over items of 42.40 and 236.09, which make
% 278.49, and the cheque sides with the subtotal. Leaf 125 rules off 2700.00 over
% two oils of 1200 each, and leaf 126 carries that 2700 forward inside its
% « from preceeding page total » of 4790.00, so subtotal and carry-forward side
% against the items by 300. Leaf 123's entry of 27 June names thirteen things and
% prices twelve of them: 1500, 360, 500 and 300 of etchings make 2660 against a
% subtotal of 2760, and the hundred is the Drawing, which has no figure. Leaf
% 128's commission line of 1333.30 leaves a net of 2666.67 where 2666.70 is what
% it subtracts to.
%
% The leaf and year sums go on being the best check in the volume and three of
% them refuse. Exact: leaf 120's 5341.57 for 1946, leaf 121's 2360.53 for 1947
% (written as « 1800 + 560 53 » and then added), leaf 122's 5.66, leaf 124's
% « 1949 total 3661.14 » - which is struck across three leaves and is the second
% year total in Book IV to be labelled with its year - leaf 126's 350.00, leaf
% 127's 4870.00 for 1951 (4520 plus leaf 126's 350), leaf 129's 4115.77 and leaf
% 131's 6993.48. Leaf 128's 7245.26 is ten short of its own receipts and the ten
% is the N.Y. Times reproduction fee, which leaf 127 had counted twice over. Leaf
% 130's 4030.07 is a dollar over, in the dollars and not the cents, which is the
% slip of leaves 83 and 86 in batches 7 and 8. And leaf 121's foot carries a
% pencil 6339.04 that answers to nothing on the leaf or the two beside it. 1948
% and 1952 are never totalled anywhere.
%
% Two habits arrive and one returns. A buyer can be named in brackets in the red
% receipt line - « rec'd by check (Zucker) » and « rec'd by check (Clancey) » on
% leaf 131 - which the volume has not done before. A pencil cross stands in the
% money column beside two of the largest Rehn cheques, on leaves 128 and 129;
% batch 10 found the same mark on leaf 116 under the words « not included in
% income », and these two carry no words at all, so nothing is claimed for them
% here. And the medium letters of batch 10 run the whole batch - « o » or « oil »,
% « w.c », « w,c », « w.C », and « E » for etching, used on some entries and not
% on others.
%
% Spellings, all as written: « Sattillo Roof Tops » on leaf 121 against
% « Saltillo Mansion » and « Palms at Saltillo » on leaf 120; « preceeding » on
% leaf 126; « Am. Artists Group, inc » and « Am. Artist Group, inc » eight lines
% apart on leaf 122, against « American Artists Group » on leaf 121; « Lombard
% House » on leaf 125 against « Lombards House » on leaf 130; « Night in the
% Parks » on leaf 125 against « Night in the Park » on leaves 123, 124 and 126;
% « Ryders House » and « Cobbs Barns » without apostrophes; « Frank H. M. Rehn »
% once on leaf 130 where every other line of the batch gives K. M.
