% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-06 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human. Its own context, no batch before it in this conversation.
%
% Book II, batch 3: sheets 25 to 36, leaves 33 and 35 to 44. Leaf 33 is
% photographed twice, so the batch covers eleven leaves. Leaf 34 was not
% photographed and does not appear.
%
% Four things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% Book II is not shaped like Book I and this batch is where the difference is
% plainest. There is no ruled table with headings anywhere in it. A leaf is one
% to three work blocks, each of them a title and a size and a date in ink, then
% several sentences of Jo Hopper's description, then a single sale line - buyer,
% price less commission, net, date of the check - and beside each block one of
% Edward Hopper's ink record drawings, ruled into a box. Eleven of the twelve
% sketches in the batch carry no writing at all. The sale lines do fall into
% columns, consistently down each leaf, and those are set as one-row tables
% with no headings, because she wrote none.
%
% Two things are pasted to these leaves and both cover text. On leaf 33 the
% Cranbrook Life clipping lies over the left half of the Ground Swell drawing;
% sheet 17990, which the Whitney calls « Verso » though what it photographs is
% the recto again, shows the drawing whole and the same text below it, so the
% transcription is there and sheet 17601 carries the clipping and a note. On
% leaf 39 a separate pencil drawing is taped over the Cottages at Wellfleet
% entry and covers most of four lines of it - the buyer, the price, the date,
% and the collection. Nothing turns that one back. Those lines are \ill{} and
% the \ill{} means « under paper ».
%
% She corrects herself constantly here, above the line rather than in the
% margin: « ed » over « decidly », « Should be South » over a title that says
% North, « Cape Cod » over a struck « Truro », « greenish » over « Grey roof
% tiles ». Every one of those is given as a \marginal{} after the passage it
% stands over, saying which words it sits above. That convention is this
% batch's, and it is stated on leaf 36 where the first of them falls.
%
% The subject is the Cape Cod watercolours and it barely moves. Leaves 36 to 44
% are nine consecutive summers at South Truro, 1932 to 1936, at 14 x 20 or
% 20 x 28, sold through Rehn at 400 to 750 less a third - Wadsworth Atheneum,
% Fogg, Whitney, Worcester, Des Moines, Addison at Andover. The two leaves in
% front of them are not that: leaf 33 is Ground Swell, finished in 1939 and
% bought by the Corcoran out of its 1943 biennial for 2000, and leaf 35 is
% Blackwell's Island of 1928, which Book I records too, at 2500 - the note on
% the leaf says so.
