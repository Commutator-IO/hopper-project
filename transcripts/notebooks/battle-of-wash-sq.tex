% unchecked against the photographs by a human.
%
% Battle of Wash Sq.: a pocket memorandum book of eight photographed sheets,
% the whole of it read in one sitting. It is not a diary kept day by day. Page 1
% is a list of the people in the Washington Square fight of 1947, page 2
% another, page 3 the one dated entry - a meeting of the Washington Square
% Neighbors on 30 November 1948 - which runs on to the head of page 4, and
% pages 5 and 6 are memoranda of demolitions, deaths and degrees added in ink
% and then in pencil down to 1955. Page 7 and pages 8-9 carry no writing but
% the stub of a leaf cut out between 8 and 9; page 10 has two pencil lines,
% one of them upside down. \entry{} is used only where a date heads a passage,
% which is twice; the lists are given line for line as she wrote them.
