% Book I, batch 2: sheets 13 to 24, leaves 6 to 16. The etchings proper, five
% plates to eleven leaves - Night Shadows, East Side Interior, The Cat Boat,
% The Rail Road, The Locomotive, The Monhegan Boat - each opening on an odd
% leaf and running onto the next.
%
% Three things about this batch that the first did not have.
%
% The column headings are written out here, and they differ from leaf to leaf:
% leaf 6 has « Date / accepted Refused / Exhibitions » and then, halfway down,
% a second heading band for the resale block - « Sold by / to whom / amt. rec'd
% minus Com. / Date of check rec'd ». Leaf 8 rules six columns and names them
% all. They are transcribed as she wrote them, per leaf, and not normalised
% into one scheme.
%
% Leaf 9 is photographed twice, and the second photograph is the useful one:
% the Pittsburgh clipping is hinged, and sheet 17703 shows it turned back with
% the rows it covers legible beneath. The table is therefore transcribed once,
% at 17703, and 18226 carries the clipping and a note.
%
% Leaves 11, 13 and 15 are almost empty - four lines, one line, two lines. They
% are transcribed as they stand. A leaf ruled for forty entries and carrying
% one is a fact about how the book was kept.
