% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-06 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human. Its own context, no batch before it in this conversation.
%
% Book I, batch 6: sheets 61 to 72, leaves 56 to 63. Leaves 57 and 59 are each
% photographed twice, and the opening of leaves 58 and 59 twice more, so the
% batch covers eight leaves.
%
% Four things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% Leaf 56 is the most nearly lost leaf in Book I so far. Three things are
% pasted over it - a photograph across the head, an exhibition catalogue leaf
% across the middle, a newspaper cutting across the foot - and between them
% they cover every column the leaf rules. What survives is the heading, one
% date, one consignment note, the first letter of a title, one entry that
% happens to fall in the gap between two clippings, and the words « When
% rec'd. » at the gutter. Everything else on that leaf is \ill{}, and the
% \ill{} means « under paper », not « faint ».
%
% Leaf 57 repeats the problem batch 5 met at leaf 53, and repeats its answer.
% The Night Windows halftone is hinged: on sheet 17751 it lies flat over the
% foot of the leaf, and on sheet 18424 it has been turned back, whereupon its
% own verso - an advertisement for Easter eggs - lands on the right-hand
% columns. Neither photograph shows the leaf whole. The transcription is at
% 18424, which shows the more, and the two figures that only 17751 shows are
% given there with a note saying where they were read.
%
% Leaf 59 is the same arrangement with a happier outcome: on sheet 18455 the
% Tables for Ladies halftone lies over the entry, and on 16991 it has been
% turned back clear of the leaf altogether. The transcription is at 16991,
% where nothing is hidden.
%
% The subject changes at leaf 60 and the prices fall back an order of
% magnitude. Leaves 56 to 59 are the oils of 1928 to 1930 - Blackwell's Island
% at 2500, Tables for Ladies to the Metropolitan at 4500, Early Sunday Morning
% to Mrs. Whitney at 3000. Leaves 60 to 63 go back five years to the
% watercolours of the two Gloucester summers, and there almost every line is
% 100 dollars, in October 1924, out of the show that made him. The leaves were
% written up long afterwards: the same rows carry resale figures of 1957 and
% 1962 in a later ink.
