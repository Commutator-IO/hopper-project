% PROVENANCE CAVEAT. The skill asks for a fresh context per batch, because a
% pass reading twelve leaves degrades towards the end with nothing to signal
% it. This batch and those after it were read in one long session covering the
% whole of Book I, which is what the rule warns against. It is recorded here
% rather than left for a reader to discover: batches 1 and 2 each had a context
% of their own, and batches 3 onward did not.
%
% Book I, batch 3: sheets 25 to 36, leaves 17 to 32. Seven plates - House by a
% River, Maine Coast, Aux Fortifications, Railroad Crossing, The Lonely House,
% American Landscape, Night in the L Train, House Tops - and three leaves that
% carry a clipping and almost nothing else.
%
% Two things about this run. The leaves are shorter than leaves 6 to 16: these
% are the plates that sold in tens rather than in hundreds, and several stop
% after a dozen entries. And the second heading band - « Date of sale / amt.
% rec'd / Sold by whom - to whom » - is ruled and named here on leaves 18, 20
% and 26, where batch 2 had to infer it.
%
% Leaf 28 is photographed twice. The clipping over its upper right is hinged;
% sheet 18395 shows it turned back, so the table is transcribed there and 18500
% carries the clipping and a note - the same arrangement leaf 9 needed.
