% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-08 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book III, batch 8: sheets 85 to 90 - the last batch of the volume, and a
% short one. Book III holds ninety sheets, so this batch is six and not twelve:
% five leaves headed Memoranda, printed 190 to 194, and the back cover.
%
% Seven things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% This is not a work book and it is not a list. Batch 2 found Book III's first
% nineteen sheets to be standing lists and everything from sheet 20 to be one
% picture to a leaf; these five leaves are neither. They are continuous prose,
% written as memoranda, and they are the densest writing in the volume by a
% wide margin - leaf 192 carries fifty-five lines where the Whereabouts leaves
% of batch 7 carried twenty-eight. There is no ruled table anywhere in the
% batch, no record drawing, no paint formula, and only four figures of money on
% five leaves. Budget the whole of the attention for prose.
%
% The back cover carries no writing of any kind, only the stationer's printed
% border, so it has no entry here; the gap in the sequence is the record, as
% Book I's leaf 117 and Book V's sheet 16 were left. Book III therefore closes
% at 89 transcribed sheets of 90.
%
% Full-width bands are the only way to read these leaves. Leaf 190 reads at
% 300% in bands 190 px deep stepping 140; leaves 191 and 192 need 340% and 380%
% and a step of 120 to 125, because their lines run at thirty-four pixels where
% leaf 190's run at fifty. The overlap is what makes a line falling on a seam
% readable from the band below, and on leaf 192 it is not optional.
%
% Two of batch 7's unsettled readings are settled by leaf 192, by her own hand
% rather than by inference. Batch 7 could not fix the name of the man who drove
% the Hoppers back to New York to vote - « Carl Gross », where « Gnes » fitted
% the strokes as well; leaf 192 writes the name in the line and then writes
% « Gross » above it as her own correction. Batch 7 also left « El » plus a
% struck word plus « French » on leaf 171; leaf 192 writes « Elwood French »
% plainly. Both are recorded below and in the reference.
%
% The dates do not run forwards and one of them is 1967. Leaf 192 goes 1961,
% 1967, 1962, 1961 in four consecutive lines, and the 1967 - the death of
% Arnold Slade of Truro - is the latest date anywhere in Book III and the year
% Edward Hopper died. The seven is crossed, which is what distinguishes it from
% her one. It is not corrected and not moved.
%
% A mark that will not settle recurs across two leaves. Leaf 192 gives Col.
% Richard Magee as « of air force, 2. B. » and leaf 190's left-margin note puts
% the same two characters against the same man - « the Col. Dick Magee ... 2.
% B's ... on Beacon St, Boston ». Neither resolves, and the point of recording
% both is that they are the same mark and not two accidents.
%
% Long horizontal strokes are still t-bars, as batch 7 found on leaf 123. On
% leaf 190 the stroke that appears to cancel « Retrospective » is the crossbar
% of its own t running out to the right, and the same is true of « Construction
% » and of « water color » on the line below it. What is genuinely struck on
% these leaves is scribbled over rather than ruled through, and it is scribbled
% past reading every time: five of the batch's struck passages are \ill{}.
%
% Leaf 190 restates, as a memorandum, an exchange that batch 6's leaf 107 had
% struck out. There the Whitney's taking of Construction in Mexico in exchange
% for Lombard House stands cancelled on the work's own leaf; here it is written
% out again with the sizes and the 1934 price, and called an even exchange.
% The two are not merged and neither is corrected from the other.
