% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-08 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book III, batch 5: sheets 49 to 60 - twelve leaves of the work book, running
% from leaf 55 to leaf 77 on the odd numbers, with no written verso among them.
%
% Eight things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% The shape batch 4 described holds: one oil to a leaf, Edward's ink record
% drawing at the head, then the title and the size, then where and when it was
% painted, then the canvas and the colours, then her description, then the
% sale. No ruled table, no clipping, no verso, no leaf the Whitney left
% unnumbered. What changes is the money and the prose, and both change a great
% deal.
%
% The colourman changes name and the leaf records it without comment. Batch 4
% had eleven leaves of Winsor \& Newton (National) canvas. Leaf 55 is the last
% of those. From leaf 57 the parenthesis reads Herga, spelt out in quotation
% marks from leaf 63 onward and unmistakable there; leaf 65 breaks the run once
% with W \& N. Winton Canvas, which this volume has used only once before, on
% leaf 1 in 1943, and which Book II uses throughout. Along
% with it the canvas turns from double prime to single prime - and on four
% leaves, 61, 63, 65 and 73, the word « single » is written and then struck,
% while on 69, 71, 75 and 77 it is written and left standing. Nothing on any
% leaf says why.
%
% The white moves the other way and settles. Chremnitz White to leaf 61,
% Crimnitz on leaf 61 itself, then Flake White from leaf 69 to the end. Leaf 67
% is the exception that is not one: the picture was painted about 1932 and she
% writes « Colors and canvas unknown, probably zinc white », which is the only
% place in either batch where she admits she does not know what is on a canvas.
%
% The money stops being one line. Batch 4 had a single worked column in twelve
% leaves; this batch has three, and they are the most interesting things on
% their leaves. Leaf 69 sets two payments on account against a price of 14 500
% and shows them summing to the commission. Leaf 73 works a museum purchase
% through two commissions - the Whitney's own sixth and Rehn's third - down to
% 8 333.34 and then splits that across two payments thirteen months apart. Leaf
% 75 braces two receipts against a total of 15 000 and takes the third off at
% the foot. Every figure in the batch reconciles, and none of the arithmetic
% has been checked into the transcription: 7000 - 1/3 = 4666 2/3, 5000 - 1/3 =
% 3333 1/3, 3500 - 1/3 = 2333 1/3, 6000 - 1/3 = 4000, 6500 - 1/3 = 4333 1/3,
% 9000 - 1/3 = 6000, 10 000 - 1/3 = 6666.67, 14 500 - 1/3 = 9666 2/3, 15 000 -
% 1/3 = 10 000 twice, 16 500 - 1/3 = 11 000.
%
% The prices roughly treble across the batch. Leaf 57 sells at 5000 in 1958 and
% leaf 77 at 16 500 in 1963, and the leaves in between climb steadily without
% ever remarking on it.
%
% Three leaves carry an anecdote rather than a description, and they are the
% best of the batch. Leaf 65 records that Stephen Clark climbed the
% seventy-four steps of 3 Washington Square North to see the canvas in the
% studio. Leaf 69 records that he tried to do the same for Excursion into
% Philosophy, found the gallery moved and locked, wrote to say so, and died -
% and she closes « It should have been his picture. » Leaf 75 records that the
% producers of the Diary of Anne Frank sent a donation to the Friends Society
% of Philadelphia at Christmas 1965 in the Hoppers' name. None of it is a
% ledger entry and all of it is on the leaf.
%
% She calls Edward « Ett » on leaf 75, the only pet name for him anywhere in
% the volumes so far, and quotes him calling the woman in the canvas « a wise
% tramp ». The pencil alternative title « the WISE TRAMP » is drawn in mixed
% capitals under the inked title of the same leaf and is not attributed here.
%
% Two leaves, 61 and 73, carry their record drawing on a plain sheet laid over
% the leaf, its lower edge visible above the title line, as leaf 1 of this
% volume does with its Croxley sheet. The leaf's own printed foliation shows
% above the sheet. On leaf 73 the sizes 40 and 50 are pencilled on the leaf
% outside the sheet's edge.
%
% Her titles are in quotation marks on ten of the twelve leaves; leaves 61 and
% 73 underline instead. Following batches 3 and 4, the marks and the underlines
% are not carried into \work{} - they are noted here rather than silently
% dropped. The year stands in the head margin of every leaf above the ruling,
% in pencil on most and in ink on leaves 57, 67 and 77; leaf 77 alone writes
% « Painted 1962 » rather than a bare year, and leaf 67 writes « ± 1932. Sky
% 1959. » for a canvas repainted twenty-seven years after it was begun.
