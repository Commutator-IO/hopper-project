% Pass: Opus 5 (claude-opus-5), 2026-09-07 - first pass, unchecked against the
% sheets by a human.
%
% Book III, batch 1: sheets 1 to 12 - the front cover, the title page, a pasted
% photograph, the index, three Whereabouts leaves, two Prizes and Museum
% Purchases leaves and three of the eight Reviews and Reproductions leaves.
%
% Six things worth knowing before reading it.
%
% Not one sheet in the batch carries a leaf number, and that is right rather
% than a gap: the catalogue records every one of the first nineteen sheets of
% Book III as unnumbered, and the volume's own foliation does not begin until
% Hotel Lobby at leaf 1. Every \sheet{} in this file therefore takes an empty
% second argument.
%
% No leaf here is ruled into columns. The stationer ruled these pages
% horizontally and nothing else, and she writes down them in running prose with
% dates at the left - so the batch has one ledgertable in it, on the last sheet,
% where she sets a hundred picture titles in four columns of her own making.
% Everything else is transcribed as lines.
%
% The front of Book III is fifteen leaves of standing lists, not works. The
% volume is the last of the three work books and it runs to Hopper's death, but
% it opens with Whereabouts, Prizes and Museum Purchases, eight leaves of
% Reviews and Reproductions, Gifts and One Man Shows, and only then begins the
% paintings. This batch is the first eleven of those list leaves and no picture
% record at all, which is why there is no \sketch{} in the file.
%
% The volume has a printed alphabetical thumb index cut into its fore-edge, and
% the index leaf uses it. Against « Prizes, Museum Purchases » she writes « G H
% », against « Reproductions + Reviews » « M Mc », against « Gifts » « V », and
% against « Summer Whereabouts » « C D ». Those are tab letters, not page
% numbers, and the tabs are visible along the right edge of sheet 17834. She
% gives Book I page numbers in the same rows, because these lists continue lists
% begun there.
%
% She spells the first of those lists three ways in four sheets:
% « Whereboats » in the index, « Where bouts » in the heading of the leaf
% itself, « Whereabouts » at the head of sheet 17707. All three stand as
% written.
%
% Her red pencil takes a new job here. In Book I it rings a figure and
% underlines a total; on the two Reviews and Reproductions leaves it underlines
% every picture title, so that a leaf of magazine references can be read down
% its titles. It is not a correction and it does not survive into the
% transcription except where a note says what it marks.
%
% The subject is the middle of the career seen from outside it: the Rehn show of
% January 1948 and its reviews, the museum purchases of 1945 to 1963, the 1950
% Whitney retrospective and its journey to Boston and Detroit, the honorary
% degrees, the Gold Medal, the Huntington Hartford fellowship - and, on the
% three Whereabouts leaves, fifty years of summers at Gloucester, Two Lights,
% South Truro and Mexico, with the hospital stays and the fights over the
% electricity and Washington Square set down in the same hand and the same ink
% as the prizes.
