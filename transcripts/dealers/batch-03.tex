%
% Dealers/Etchings, batch 3: twelve sheets, leaves 75 to 87. All twelve carry
% writing, all twelve are transcribed, no opening is photographed twice, and -
% as in batches 1 and 2 - there is not one clipping in the batch.
%
% Four things about these twelve leaves.
%
% First, the batch spans leaves 75 to 87 but only twelve sheets, because leaf
% 79 was not photographed. The printed numbers run 75, 76, 77, 78, then 80: the
% gap is in the Whitney's sequence, not in the book, and nothing here says what
% is on the missing leaf. Batch 2 had the same shape at 64 and 65.
%
% Second, the volume is now entirely what leaf 51's index promised. One dealer
% to a leaf: 75 and 82 are the Downtown Gallery, 76 and 77 the Weyhe Book Shop,
% 78 the Whitney Studio Club, 80 and 81 E. P. Jennings, 83 Kleemann-Thorman, 84
% Keppel continued from 72, 85 Kennedy continued from 73, 86 Vickery, Atkins
% & Tory, and 87 the private sales that belong to no dealer at all. The
% pointers batch 2 found at the foot of each leaf are here on both sides:
% « Continued p. 82 » closes leaf 75 and « Continued from p. 75 » opens leaf 82.
%
% Third, three leaves stop being a table and become an argument. Leaf 77 tells
% what happened when the Weyhe etchings came back to 3 Washington Square in
% 1943 while the Hoppers were in Mexico - four prints missing, one extra, and
% the extra one they were not supposed to have. Leaf 82 closes the Downtown
% Gallery account by reconciling two inventories that disagree. Leaf 84 works
% out that of thirty-nine prints received from Harlow only twenty-eight are on
% record, and where the other eleven went. All three are Josephine Hopper
% auditing her own book, and they are the best writing in the batch.
%
% Fourth, the money on leaf 85 is thirty years later than the money on leaf 80.
% Jennings sold etchings at ten dollars in 1925; Kennedy sold them at fifty in
% 1956, and by then the Hoppers were being told about the sales after the fact
% - « we not consulted », « We in Mexico, not consulted », « Don't know when
% Kennedy got it. »
