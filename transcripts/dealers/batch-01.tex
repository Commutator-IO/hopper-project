% sheets by a human.
%
% Dealers/Etchings, batch 1: the twelve sheets from the front cover to leaf 60.
% All twelve carry writing and all twelve are transcribed; no leaf is blank, no
% opening is photographed twice, and there is not a single clipping in the
% batch.
%
% Two things about this volume that the other five do not prepare you for.
%
% First, the leaf numbers are PRINTED, in blue, by the stationer - at the top
% right of a recto and the top left of a verso, so that a leaf carries two
% numbers, one per side. That is why the volume "begins at 51": it does not
% begin anywhere, it is the back half of a book whose front was used for
% something else. It also changes what a missing number means. Sheets 3 and 4
% have no number not because nobody wrote one but because the corner carrying
% it is torn away, and through the tear on sheet 3 you can read the 53 printed
% on the leaf beneath. The Whitney supplies [51] and [52] in brackets from the
% sequence, and the catalogue records them as unnumbered; this file follows the
% catalogue and says so where it matters.
%
% Second, the volume is indexed by whose hands the work was in. Leaf 51 ends
% with two little tables of dealers against leaf numbers - Keppel 70, Krashaar
% 72, Kennedy 85 + 73 - and those numbers are the plan of everything after
% leaf 69. The first ten leaves here are not that. They are the watercolours,
% the Paris oils and drawings, and five oils recorded one to a block, and they
% run out at leaf 60 into a list of exhibitions.
